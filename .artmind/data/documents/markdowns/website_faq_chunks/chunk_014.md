# REGULATORY & SAFETY  
**Q: Is FirstUK Bank regulated?**
A: Yes, by the FCA (Financial Conduct Authority) and PRA (Prudential Regulation Authority).  
**Q: What if FirstUK Bank fails?**
A: Your deposits (up to £85,000) are protected by FSCS.  
**Q: How can I verify FirstUK is real?**
A: Check FCA register at www.register.fca.org.uk (search "FirstUK Bank").  
**Q: What if I have a data breach concern?**
A: Contact our Data Protection Officer: dpo@firstuk.bank
