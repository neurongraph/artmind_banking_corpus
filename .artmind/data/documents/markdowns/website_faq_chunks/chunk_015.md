# GETTING STARTED  
**Q: How do I open an account?**
A: Visit www.firstuk.bank/open-account to start online (fastest), or call 0800-555-2265.  
**Q: What's the first step?**
A: Choose a product (SmartSaver, Current, or Mortgage) and click "Open Account."  
**Q: When can I use my account?**
A: Immediately after opening (online/branch), or 5–7 days (postal).
