# BRANCHES & LOCATIONS  
**Q: Where are FirstUK branches?**
A: 12 branches across the UK:
- **London:** Canary Wharf + Head Office (Threadneedle Street)
- **North West:** Manchester, Liverpool
- **Midlands:** Birmingham
- **North East:** Leeds
- **South:** Bristol, Southampton
- **Scotland:** Edinburgh, Glasgow
- **East:** Norwich
- **Ireland:** Belfast  
**Q: What are branch opening hours?**
A: Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00, Saturday 09:00–13:00 (closed Sundays/bank holidays).  
**Q: Can I visit without an appointment?**
A: Yes, walk-ins welcome. For complex matters, book an appointment (call 0800-555-2265).
