# COMPLAINTS & SUPPORT  
**Q: What if I'm unhappy with FirstUK Bank?**
A: We want to fix it. Contact us:
- **Phone:** 0800-555-2265
- **Email:** support@firstuk.bank
- **Branch:** Visit any location  
**Q: What's the complaint process?**
A:
1. Report issue to us (phone/email/branch)
2. We investigate (typically 8 days)
3. We respond with decision and next steps
4. If unresolved, escalate to Financial Ombudsman Service (free)  
**Q: How long does FirstUK take to respond?**
A: Within 8 calendar days (or up to 8 weeks if complex).  
**Q: Can I complain to Financial Ombudsman?**
A: Yes, if you're unhappy with our response. They're free and independent.
- **Phone:** 0800 023 4567
- **Website:** www.financialombudsman.org.uk
