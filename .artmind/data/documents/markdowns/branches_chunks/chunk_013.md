## Branch Services  
### Standard Services (All Branches)  
- **Account Opening:** SmartSaver, Current Account (see [[products]])
- **Account Queries:** Balance, transaction history, product information
- **Cash Services:** Deposits, withdrawals, change
- **Card Services:** Debit card replacement, blocking, PIN reset
- **Standing Orders & Direct Debits:** Setup, modification, cancellation
- **Complaint Resolution:** First-line complaint handling
- **Mortgage Inquiries:** Information, application support
