## Training & Development  
**New Branch Staff Training:**
- Banking fundamentals (2 days)
- Product knowledge (1 day, see [[products]])
- Customer service (1 day)
- Compliance & AML (1 day, see [[departments]])
- Cash handling & security (1 day)
- Systems training (1 day)  
**Ongoing Training:**
- Compliance training (annual)
- Product updates (quarterly)
- Customer service coaching (monthly)  
**See:** Product Training Manual (PTM-001), Branch Operations Training (BOT-001)
