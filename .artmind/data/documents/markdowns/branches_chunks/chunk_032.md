## Business Continuity  
**Branch Closure Procedures:**
- 30-day customer notice required
- Customer data transfer support
- Alternative branch assignment
- Digital services promotion  
**Disaster Recovery:**
- Banking services rerouted to nearest branch
- Customer data secured in off-site backup
- Communication plan activation
- Regular BCP testing (annual)  
**See:** Business Continuity Plan (BCP-001)
