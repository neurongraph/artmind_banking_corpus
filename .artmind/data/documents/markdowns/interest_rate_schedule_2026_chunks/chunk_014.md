## HOW TO MAXIMIZE INTEREST EARNED  
### Strategy 1: Move Funds to Higher Tier  
**Scenario:** You have £11,000
```
If kept in Tier 1 (< £10k):
- Only first £10,000 earns 4.50%
- Interest earned: £450/year

If moved to Tier 2 (£10k–£50k):
- All £11,000 earns 4.70%
- Interest earned: £517/year
- **Gain: +£67/year** (just by moving to correct tier)
```
