## CURRENT RATES (Effective January 15, 2026)  
### SmartSaver Account (SAV-001)  
**Variable Interest Rates by Balance Tier:**  
| Tier | Balance Range | Annual Interest Rate (AER) |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | £0–£10,000 | **4.50%** |
| **Tier 2** | £10,001–£50,000 | **4.70%** |
| **Tier 3** | £50,001+ | **4.80%** |  
**Interest Accrual:** Daily (calculated on account balance)
**Interest Payment:** Monthly (last business day of month)
**Rate Type:** Variable (reviewed monthly)  
**Variation Basis:**
- Base: Bank of England Base Rate
- Margin: +2.50% (fixed)
- Current BoE Base Rate: 4.00% (as of 2026-01-15)  
**Calculation Example (30-day month):**
```
Balance: £10,000 (Tier 1)
Daily Rate: 4.50% ÷ 365 days = 0.01233%
Monthly Interest: £10,000 × 0.01233% × 30 days = £36.99
Paid on: Last business day of month
```
