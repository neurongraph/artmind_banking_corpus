## Your Complaint Rights Summary  
| Right | Details |
|---|---|
| **Free Complaint** | No charge to lodge or escalate |
| **8-Day Response** | We respond within 8 calendar days |
| **Full Investigation** | Thorough review of your issue |
| **Clear Explanation** | We explain our findings in detail |
| **Compensation** | If we're wrong, we compensate you |
| **Ombudsman Appeal** | Escalate to Financial Ombudsman if unhappy |
| **6-Month Window** | You have 6 months to escalate to Ombudsman |
| **No Retaliation** | We won't penalize you for complaining |
