verification (KYC - Know Your Customer).

Choose one:
- UK Passport (copy is fine)
- UK Driving License (copy is fine)

PLUS one of these for your address:
- Utility bill (dated within 3 months)
- Council tax letter

How to Submit:
1. Upload securely: www.firstuk.bank/kyc-upload
2. Email: kyc@firstuk.bank
3. Visit a branch in person

Deadline: [date - typically 30 days]

Why This Matters:
Regulatory requirements ensure we're banking responsibly and
protecting your account.

Questions?
- Phone: 0800-555-2265
- Email: support@firstuk.bank

Thank you for your cooperation!

FirstUK Bank Compliance Team
