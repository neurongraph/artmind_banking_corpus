## Template 3: Rate Change Notification  
**Subject:** Your Interest Rate Has Changed

Dear [Customer Name],

The Bank of England has changed its base rate, and your FirstUK
Bank interest rate has been updated.

What Changed:
- Previous Rate: [old rate]%
- New Rate: [new rate]%
- Effective Date: [date]

Why Did It Change?
Your SmartSaver rate is variable, which means it moves with the
Bank of England base rate. We maintain a fixed 2.5% margin above
BoE, so you're always competitive.

What This Means:
Your monthly interest will change starting next month. If rates
increased, you'll earn more. If they decreased, you'll earn less.

Example Calculation:
- Balance: £10,000
- Old Interest: ~£37.50/month
- New Interest: ~[new amount]/month

Not Happy With the Change?
If you'd prefer a fixed rate, we recommend our Fixed Rate Bonds
(coming 2026). Sign up for notifications at www.firstuk.bank/bonds

Thank you for being a FirstUK Bank customer.
