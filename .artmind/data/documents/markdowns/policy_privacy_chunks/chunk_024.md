### Data Controllers (Receive Data)  
**Recipients of Customer Data:**
- Regulatory authorities (FCA, PRA, NCA)
- Law enforcement (if court order)
- Credit reference agencies (if credit product)
- Third-party service providers (with DPA)  
**Data Sharing Restrictions:**
- Only shared where lawful basis exists
- Shared only with necessary recipients
- International transfers (to EEA countries only, currently)
