| Purpose | Lawful Basis | Data Retained |
|---------|-------------|----------------|

| KYC/AML compliance | Legal obligation | 5-6 years post-closure |
| Fraud detection | Legitimate interest | 3 years |
| Marketing (if consented) | Explicit consent | Until withdrawal |
| Regulatory reporting | Legal obligation | Per regulation (5-7 years) |
| Complaint handling | Contractual & legal | 8 weeks (FCA requirement) |
