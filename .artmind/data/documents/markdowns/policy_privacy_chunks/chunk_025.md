## Retention Schedules  
**See [[policy_retention]] for detailed retention by data type.**  
**Standard Retention Summary:**  
| Data Type | Retention Period | Basis |
|-----------|------------------|-------|
| Customer profile | Account tenure + 6 years | POCA, anti-fraud |
| Transaction records | 6 years minimum | Regulatory, dispute |
| KYC/AML records | 5-6 years post-closure | AML regulations |
| Complaint records | 6 years minimum | Regulatory |
| Marketing consent | Until withdrawal | GDPR consent |
| Website cookies | Session (+ 14 months analytics) | Cookie consent |
