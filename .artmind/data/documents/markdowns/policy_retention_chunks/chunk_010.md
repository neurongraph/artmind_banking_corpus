### Mortgage-Specific Documents  
**Mortgages require extended retention due to security (property lien).**  
| Document Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| **Mortgage Application** | Life of loan + 6 years |
| **Underwriting File** | Life of loan + 6 years |
| **Property Valuation** | Life of loan + 6 years |
| **Legal Charge/Deed** | Life of loan + 6 years |
| **Payment Records** | Life of loan + 6 years |
