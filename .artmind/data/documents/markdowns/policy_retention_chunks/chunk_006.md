## Retention Schedule by Document Type  
### Customer & Account Documents  
| Document Type | Retention Period | Legal Basis | Destruction Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer Profile** | Account tenure + 6 years | POCA, AML, dispute | Shred/delete |
| **KYC/CDD Records** | 5 years post-closure | Money Laundering Regs 2017 | Shred/delete |
| **Identification Docs** | 6 years post-closure | POCA, anti-fraud | Shred/delete |
| **Address Verification** | 6 years post-closure | KYC requirements | Shred/delete |
| **Account Opening Forms** | 6 years post-closure | Contract evidence | Shred/delete |
| **Statements** | Account tenure + 6 years | Customer service, disputes | Shred/delete |
| **Transaction Records** | 6 years minimum | POCA, audit, disputes | Shred/delete |
| **Standing Orders** | Account tenure + 2 years | Contract evidence | Delete |
| **Direct Debit Mandates** | 6 years post-cancellation | DD scheme rules | Shred/delete |
