### Operational & Support Documents  
| Document Type | Retention Period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **SOPs & Procedures** | Current + 2 prior versions | Governance, audit trail |
| **Policy Documents** | Permanent (all versions) | Governance, legal |
| **Training Records** | 3 years | Compliance training verification |
| **Staff Disciplinary** | 6 years from action | Employment law |
| **System Access Logs** | 1 year | Security, audit |
| **Backup Tapes** | 30 days (rotating) | Disaster recovery |
| **Email (general)** | 1–3 years | Business efficiency |
| **Email (legal/compliance)** | 7 years | Legal hold |
