### Compliance & Regulatory Documents  
| Document Type | Retention Period | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Complaint Files** | 6 years from resolution | FCA COBS Part 2 |
| **Regulatory Reports** | 5 years | PRA Rulebook, audit |
| **AML Screening Results** | 5 years post-closure | Money Laundering Regs |
| **SAR (Suspicious Activity)** | 5 years | Proceeds of Crime Act |
| **Audit Reports** | 7 years | Statutory audit |
| **Board/Committee Minutes** | Permanent | Corporate governance |
| **Compliance Bulletins** | 3 years | Regulatory reference |
| **Regulatory Circulars** | 3–5 years | Regulatory guidance |
