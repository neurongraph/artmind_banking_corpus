## Enhanced Due Diligence for High-Risk Customers  
A high-risk customer must receive enhanced due diligence before the account is
activated or an exception is approved.  
| Control | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Beneficial ownership | For entity customers, identify and verify every beneficial owner with more than 25% ownership or control |
| Source of funds | Obtain and assess evidence for an initial deposit above £10,000 |
| Screening | Screen OFAC, EU, UN, HMT, PEP, sanctions, and adverse-media sources |
| Approval | Head of Compliance approval before activation; CRO approval for any policy exception |
| Ongoing monitoring | Review high-risk customers at least quarterly rather than annually |
