### Impact Scale (1–5)  
**1 = Negligible**
- Financial: <£10k
- Customers: 1–5
- Regulatory: No impact  
**2 = Minor**
- Financial: £10k–£100k
- Customers: 5–25
- Regulatory: No formal action  
**3 = Moderate**
- Financial: £100k–£500k
- Customers: 25–100
- Regulatory: Review, minor guidance  
**4 = Major**
- Financial: £500k–£1M
- Customers: 100–500
- Regulatory: Fine, restrictions  
**5 = Critical**
- Financial: >£1M
- Customers: 500+
- Regulatory: License suspension, criminal action
