## Complaints & Support  
**Q: What if I'm unhappy with my service?**
A: Contact us:
- **Phone:** 0800-555-2265 (Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00, Sat 09:00–17:00)
- **Email:** support@firstuk.bank
- **Branch:** Visit any FirstUK branch
- **Online:** Submit complaint form at www.firstuk.bank  
**Q: What if I'm not satisfied with FirstUK Bank's response?**
A: You can escalate to:
- **Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS)**
- Phone: 0800 023 4567
- Website: www.financialombudsman.org.uk
- Free and independent  
**Q: How long does FirstUK take to respond to complaints?**
A:
- Standard: Within 8 calendar days
- Complex issues: Up to 8 weeks (we'll explain why)
