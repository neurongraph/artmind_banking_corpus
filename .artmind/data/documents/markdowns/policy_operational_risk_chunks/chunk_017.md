## Control Framework  
**Three Lines of Defense:**  
**Line 1: Frontline Management**
- Department heads responsible for risk
- Operating procedures
- Daily monitoring
- Immediate incident response  
**Line 2: Risk & Compliance**
- Risk policy and framework
- Independent risk monitoring
- Control testing
- Escalation procedures  
**Line 3: Internal Audit**
- Independent assurance
- Control testing
- Audit reports
- Follow-up verification
