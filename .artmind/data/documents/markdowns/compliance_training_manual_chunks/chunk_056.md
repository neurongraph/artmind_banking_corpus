## Assessment Quiz  
**You must score 80%+ to pass. Questions cover:**  
1. AML screening requirements
2. KYC document validity
3. GDPR customer rights
4. Fraud red flags
5. Reporting obligations
6. Consequences of violations  
**Questions (sample):**
- Q: What should you do if a customer can't provide proof of address? A: Don't open account, escalate to manager
- Q: Can you tell a customer they matched a sanctions screen? A: No, tipping-off is illegal
- Q: How long must you keep customer data? A: 6 years after account closure
- Q: What's the Direct Debit Guarantee? A: Full refund of unauthorized DD within 10 days
